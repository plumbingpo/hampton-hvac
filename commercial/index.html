Food Service Comfort

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

Hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort all interact, and a system that's undersized or failing reveals itself as a sweltering cook line and complaints at the tables. Fryers, ranges, and grills dump huge heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air out as fast as the rooftop units can make it. Our food-service work covers rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, with service scheduled around your hours.

Many restaurant comfort complaints trace back to air balance. When a kitchen runs negative, it pulls dining-room air the wrong way, sucks hot or cold outdoor air through every door, and leaves staff miserable at peak service. We balance the makeup air to the hood exhaust and document every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians are used to working in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Safe Installation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Hampton HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. Every account gets a service binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, gets a more frequent visit schedule.
